The Center for Mobility Analysis for Pandemic Prevention Strategies (MAPPS) at Brown studies how human mobility data can be used to inform real-time pandemic responses by simulating disease transmission.

As a student researcher, I helped design the sensor setup for collecting indoor mobility data and built a prototype for identifying contacts in a disease transmission model from spatio-temporal data. I explored different agent-based model setups in NetLogo and studied the emergent transmission patterns.

**Link:** [MAPPS Workshop 2023](https://mapps-brown.github.io/workshop2023/)
