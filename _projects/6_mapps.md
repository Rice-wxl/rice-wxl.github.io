The Center for Mobility Analysis for Pandemic Prevention Strategies (MAPPS) at Brown studies how human mobility data can be used to model the spread of pathogens.

As a student researcher, I helped design the sensor setup for collecting indoor mobility data and built a prototype for identifying contacts in a disease transmission model from spatio-temporal data. I experimented with agent-based modeling setups in NetLogo, studying the transmission patterns that emerge under different initializations and rules, and reviewed the literature on agent-based and physical models of pathogen dispersal.

**Link:** [MAPPS Workshop 2023](https://mapps-brown.github.io/workshop2023/)
